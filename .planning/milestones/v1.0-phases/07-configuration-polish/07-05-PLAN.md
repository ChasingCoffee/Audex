    **Fix — Adaptive seek for short files (UAT #10):**

    The bug: TranslateAccelerator hardcodes `SeekRelative(-5.0)` and `SeekRelative(5.0)`. For short samples (e.g., 2-second drum hit), 5 seconds overshoots the entire file.

    In PreviewWindow.cs SeekRelative method (around line 1534):
    - Change the method to compute adaptive seek amount from the direction and duration:
    - New signature: `public void SeekRelative(double direction)` where direction is +1.0 or -1.0.
    - Inside the method, compute: `double seekAmount = Math.Max(0.5, Math.Min(15.0, duration * 0.05)) * direction;`
    - This gives: 0.5s for files <=10s, 1.5s for 30s files, 5.0s for 100s files (typical 1:40 track), 15.0s for files >=5min.
    - The rest of the method stays the same: `double newPos = _player.CurrentPositionSeconds + seekAmount;` etc.

    In AudioPreviewHandler.cs TranslateAccelerator (around lines 622-627):
    - Change `SeekRelative(-5.0)` to `SeekRelative(-1.0)` (backward direction).
    - Change `SeekRelative(5.0)` to `SeekRelative(1.0)` (forward direction).

    Also update the tooltip text in ControlBarRenderer.GetTooltipText for HitZone.SeekBar:
    - Change from "Seek (Ctrl+Left/Right for 5s jumps -- click preview pane first)" to "Seek (Ctrl+Left/Right -- click preview pane first)" — remove the "5s" reference since it is now adaptive.
  </action>
